Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information Tidyridge collects, how we use it, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Order details you give us: your name, email address, your business description, and the competitor names or links you want researched.
  • Payment information: payments are handled by Razorpay. We receive confirmation of payment but we do not see or store your card or bank details.
  • Basic website data such as the pages you visit, used only to keep the site working.

How we use it

  • To prepare and deliver the report or product you ordered.
  • To contact you about your order and provide support.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers we need to run the business (such as Razorpay for payments and our email provider).

Keeping your data

We keep order records only as long as needed for support and for legal and tax requirements, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us what data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing tidyridge.briefs@tidyridge.in. We respond within 48 hours.
