Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Tidyridge sells digital products: competitor-pricing research reports and digital templates such as planners. This policy explains when you can cancel an order and when you can get a refund.

Cancellation

  • Custom research reports: you can cancel for a full refund any time before we start the research. Once research has started, see the refund terms below.
  • Ready-made templates and downloads: these are delivered instantly, so they cannot be cancelled once the download has been accessed.

Refunds

  • Our satisfaction guarantee: if a report you ordered is not useful or does not match what we described, email us within 7 days of delivery and we will revise it or refund your payment in full.
  • Not delivered: if we fail to deliver your report within the stated time, you are entitled to a full refund.
  • Digital downloads (templates, planners) are generally non-refundable once downloaded, except where the file is faulty or not as described, in which case we will fix it or refund you.

How to request a refund

Email tidyridge.briefs@tidyridge.in with your order details and the reason. We respond within 48 hours. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method through Razorpay, usually within 5 to 7 business days.
